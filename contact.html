Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about MediFind

How do I book an appointment through MediFind?

Booking an appointment through MediFind is simple. Search for a clinic or doctor, select your preferred healthcare provider, choose an available time slot, fill in your details, and confirm your booking. You'll receive a confirmation email with all the details of your appointment.

How can I list my clinic on MediFind?

To list your clinic on MediFind, click on the "Add Your Clinic" button at the top of the page. You'll need to create an account, provide details about your clinic, upload photos, and specify the services you offer. Our team will review your submission and approve it within 48 hours.

Are the special offers on MediFind legitimate?

Yes, all special offers on MediFind are legitimate and provided directly by the healthcare providers. We verify each offer before it's published on our platform to ensure its authenticity. Always check the validity period and terms of each offer before booking.

How can I leave a review for a clinic or doctor?

After your appointment, you'll receive an email inviting you to leave a review. Alternatively, you can visit the clinic or doctor's profile page, scroll down to the reviews section, and click on "Write a Review." You'll need to be logged in to your MediFind account to leave a review.

Is my personal information secure on MediFind?

Yes, we take data security very seriously. All personal information is encrypted and stored securely. We never share your information with third parties without your consent. You can read more about our data protection practices in our Privacy Policy.
